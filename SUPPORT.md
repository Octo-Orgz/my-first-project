## Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a security vulnerability, please follow the steps below:

1. **Do not open a public issue.**
2. Email our security team at **security@example.com** with:
   - Detailed description of the issue  
   - Steps to reproduce  
   - Potential impact  
3. We will acknowledge your report within **72 hours**.
4. Once validated, we will work on a fix and provide regular updates until resolved.

## Security Guidelines
- Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly before we have released a fix.
- Follow responsible disclosure best practices.
- Use secure channels when sharing sensitive information.

Thank you for helping keep our project safe!
